Hello, I'm Jatin

I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) and I'm genuinely fascinated by cybersecurity. I enjoy learning how networks, operating systems and applications fit together — and how they can be protected.

My goal is to keep growing into a well-rounded cybersecurity and development professional, combining practical field experience with a strong technical foundation.
